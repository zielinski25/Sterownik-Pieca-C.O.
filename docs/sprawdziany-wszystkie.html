🔑 Klucz odpowiedzi  ·  sprawdzian 1

Dla rodzica / nauczyciela — nie drukuj razem z arkuszem dla dziecka.

  1. are — My books → are. My books are red.
  2. Is — be na początku: Is Lily happy?
  3. isn't — Ana isn't late.
  4. My sister is at a party — My sister → is, nie are. My sister is at a party.
  5. is — Ana → is. Ana is in the classroom.
  6. Is — be na początku: Is Harry from Poland?
  7. isn't — My mum isn't happy.
  8. My shoes are green — My shoes → are, nie is. My shoes are green.
  9. is — My dad → is. My dad is helpful.
  10. Is — be na początku: Is my school bag small?
  11. isn't — Mr Brown isn't ready.
  12. Emile is from Poland — Emile → is, nie are. Emile is from Poland.
  13. am — I → am. I am helpful.
  14. Are — be na początku: Are my parents Polish?
  15. aren't — Mickey and Ana aren't ready.
