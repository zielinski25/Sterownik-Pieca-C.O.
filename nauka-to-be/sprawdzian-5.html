🔑 Klucz odpowiedzi  ·  sprawdzian 5

Dla rodzica / nauczyciela — nie drukuj razem z arkuszem dla dziecka.

  1. is — Mickey → is. Mickey is brave.
  2. Is — be na początku: Is Harry nice?
  3. isn't — Lily isn't British.
  4. I am friendly — I → am, nie are. I am friendly.
  5. are — My books → are. My books are interesting.
  6. Are — be na początku: Are the children British?
  7. isn't — My book isn't blue.
  8. Lena is brave — Lena → is, nie are. Lena is brave.
  9. is — My cat → is. My cat is blue.
  10. Are — be na początku: Are my parents at a party?
  11. isn't — Mickey isn't at the zoo.
  12. The film is red — The film → is, nie are. The film is red.
  13. are — Lily and Robbie → are. Lily and Robbie are young.
  14. Is — be na początku: Is my sister at the zoo?
  15. isn't — Robbie isn't on holiday.
