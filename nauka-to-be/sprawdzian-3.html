🔑 Klucz odpowiedzi  ·  sprawdzian 3

Dla rodzica / nauczyciela — nie drukuj razem z arkuszem dla dziecka.

  1. isn't — The robot isn't new.
  2. Harry is at school — Harry → is, nie are. Harry is at school.
  3. are — The students → are. The students are from Poland.
  4. Are — be na początku: Are you ready?
  5. isn't — Saskia isn't helpful.
  6. Saskia is at a party — Saskia → is, nie are. Saskia is at a party.
  7. am — I → am. I am at the cinema.
  8. Is — be na początku: Is my teacher late?
  9. isn't — My bag isn't at home.
  10. The notebooks are cool — The notebooks → are, nie is. The notebooks are cool.
  11. is — It → is. It is red.
  12. Is — be na początku: Is Robbie at a party?
  13. isn't — It isn't interesting.
  14. My jeans are nice — My jeans → are, nie is. My jeans are nice.
  15. is — My hoodie → is. My hoodie is new.
