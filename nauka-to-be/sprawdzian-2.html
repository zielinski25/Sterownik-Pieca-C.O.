🔑 Klucz odpowiedzi  ·  sprawdzian 2

Dla rodzica / nauczyciela — nie drukuj razem z arkuszem dla dziecka.

  1. Ms Collins is at a party — Ms Collins → is, nie are. Ms Collins is at a party.
  2. is — My mum → is. My mum is at school.
  3. Are — be na początku: Are we at the beach?
  4. isn't — My sister isn't at home.
  5. Lena is young — Lena → is, nie are. Lena is young.
  6. is — My dad → is. My dad is British.
  7. Is — be na początku: Is Filip brave?
  8. aren't — The notebooks aren't red.
  9. Emile is ready — Emile → is, nie are. Emile is ready.
  10. is — Ms Collins → is. Ms Collins is at school.
  11. Is — be na początku: Is Mickey funny?
  12. isn't — My friend isn't helpful.
  13. I am on holiday — I → am, nie are. I am on holiday.
  14. are — The students → are. The students are young.
  15. Are — be na początku: Are they happy?
